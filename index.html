Electrons & Compasses

Electrons spiral, bounce and drift along varied magnetic paths. Faster inner populations and slower outer populations can stay, change bands or escape, mostly through the poles. Magnetic force bends motion; the CLT trapping model supplies capture.

Motion model & references

Scaled guiding-center model with pitch-angle scattering and magnetic deflection during entry and escape. Inner-band energies and the CLT capture potential are model assumptions; this is not a calibrated plasma simulation.
