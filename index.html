Physics & references

A closed dipole field and adaptive Boris integration drive actual electron motion. Magnetic force bends velocity; the separate CLT electric potential and rotating plasma flow can change energy. Weak random pitch scattering models wave interactions. Source strength, time scales and electric terms are illustrative, not calibrated plasma parameters.
